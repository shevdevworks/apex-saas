The details, without the detour.

What does a workflow actually do?

A workflow connects a trigger to a set of rules and an action. For example, a new lead arrives, Apex matches an account owner, and a message with the lead’s details goes to Slack. Try the example above to see each step.

Can I keep a person in the loop?

That’s the idea. Approval steps let a workflow pause before an important action. In the “How it works” section, select the third step and try approving a sample release update.

How does the pricing calculator work?

Choose a monthly automation volume to see the suggested plan. Starter covers 1,000. Growth has tiers up to 25,000. Larger volumes point to Scale. Yearly billing applies a 20% discount; the yearly total is shown under the price.

Is this a live software product?

ApexSaaS is an independent design and development concept by ShevDevWorks. The workflow playground, approval interaction, and pricing calculator run in your browser using sample data. They do not connect to external accounts or process payments.
